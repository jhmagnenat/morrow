# MORROW

**An AI-powered learning environment designed to connect knowledge, strengthen understanding, and improve long-term retention.**

> **Work in Progress:** MORROW is an experimental personal project currently under development. Many features are incomplete, and the application may not function as intended.

## Overview

MORROW is a personal learning platform that brings together knowledge mapping, AI-assisted explanations, spaced repetition, language learning, and interview preparation into a single environment.

The idea behind MORROW is simple: **learning should be about building connections, understanding concepts, and remembering what matters.**

Rather than treating studying, memorization, and practice as separate activities, MORROW aims to connect them into one continuous learning process.

## Core Features

### 1. Knowledge Mapping

An interactive knowledge map inspired by Learn Anything and Topic Breakdown.

- Break down complex subjects into smaller, interconnected concepts.
- Explore relationships between topics and subtopics.
- Navigate knowledge through a visual, structured interface.
- Build a clearer understanding of how different ideas fit together.

### 2. Spaced Repetition & Active Recall

An Anki-inspired study system designed for long-term knowledge retention.

- Create and review flashcards.
- Schedule reviews using spaced repetition.
- Reinforce knowledge through active recall.
- Organize study material around specific subjects and concepts.

### 3. AI-Powered Explanations

Understand concepts from different perspectives and levels of complexity.

- **Explain Like I'm a Child:** Build intuition through simple explanations and analogies.
- **Non-Expert:** Understand the fundamental ideas without unnecessary technical complexity.
- **Expert:** Explore precise definitions, technical details, and advanced reasoning.

Explanations can also serve as a starting point for generating study material and flashcards.

### 4. English Learning

An integrated language-learning environment focused on vocabulary acquisition and pronunciation.

- Save unfamiliar English words and technical expressions.
- Access translations, definitions, and contextual examples.
- Turn vocabulary into spaced-repetition flashcards.
- Practice English pronunciation.

The objective is to develop both general English proficiency and professional vocabulary.

### 5. AI Interview Preparation

A personalized interview simulator designed to help users prepare for professional opportunities.

- Provide a job title and job description.
- Upload a CV and cover letter.
- Generate interview questions tailored to the position and candidate.
- Practice through simulated 20-minute interviews.
- Reinforce technical knowledge and improve communication skills.

## Vision: Adaptive Learning

MORROW's long-term objective is to become an adaptive learning environment that progressively understands what a user knows, what they struggle with, and what they are likely to forget.

Planned capabilities include:

- **Knowledge mastery tracking:** Visually represent proficiency through color-coded knowledge maps.
- **Weakness detection:** Identify knowledge gaps based on flashcard performance, exercises, and interview responses.
- **Personalized study sessions:** Recommend what to learn or review next.
- **Connected learning workflows:** Allow interview performance to inform study sessions, and study progress to improve interview preparation.
- **Long-term memory management:** Combine knowledge mastery estimation with spaced-repetition scheduling.

The ultimate goal is to create a continuous feedback loop:

**Explore → Understand → Practice → Remember → Apply → Improve**

## Project Status

🚧 **Early Development / Experimental**

MORROW is an independent learning and software development project.

The platform is not production-ready. Features described above represent the intended product experience and may be partially implemented, experimental, or not yet functional.

The project is being developed iteratively, with an emphasis on exploring AI-assisted education, knowledge organization, and personalized learning systems.

## Inspiration

MORROW draws inspiration from several approaches to learning and knowledge management:

- **Learn Anything** — Visual exploration of interconnected knowledge.
- **Topic Breakdown** — Structured decomposition of complex subjects.
- **Anki** — Spaced repetition and long-term retention.
- **The Feynman Technique** — Developing understanding through simple explanations.
- **Active Recall** — Strengthening memory through retrieval practice.

## Philosophy

MORROW is built around three principles:

**Understand before memorizing.** Learning begins with intuition, conceptual clarity, and meaningful connections.

**Remember through practice.** Knowledge becomes useful when it can be retrieved, explained, and applied.

**Make progress visible.** Learning should be an evolving process in which strengths, weaknesses, and improvements become increasingly clear.

---

*The name MORROW reflects the idea of tomorrow: learning today to become more capable tomorrow.*
