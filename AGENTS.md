# Product direction

Read `PRODUCT.md` before changing generation or learning flows. The intended pipeline is incomplete slides → explained and enriched course → multidimensional cards → tracked revisions. Added disciplinary context is intentional; never restore source-only restrictions to the default pipeline. Preserve the distinction between slide assertions, added background and hypothetical illustrations. Review added knowledge for correctness and relevance without pretending slide anchors prove it. Keep unresolved content out of ready cards.

The current architecture is documented in `references/teacher-course-architecture.md`. Older generation reports are historical, not product requirements. Preserve source files, edited/studied cards and revision histories. Test with disposable course data and simulated model responses; real model quality requires separate human evaluation.
